These local scene assets replace the remote stock photos used across the site.

They are lightweight SVG stand-ins based on the requested brief:
- Black African professionals
- modern office settings
- candid corporate activities
- bright daylight
- warm, professional mood

Recommended scene mapping:
- `team-meeting.svg`: conference tables, workshops, team sessions
- `desk-laptop.svg`: individual work, CV, LinkedIn, focused productivity
- `collaboration-review.svg`: two-person collaboration, reviews, coaching
- `professional-portrait.svg`: hero/about portrait treatment
- `office-lobby-group.svg`: group/lobby/team presence

If you later generate real photography with an AI image tool or commission a photographer,
you can keep the same filenames and swap the files in place without changing templates.
